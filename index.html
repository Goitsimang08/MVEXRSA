About FourSkeem Records and Production (FourSkeem P&R)

FourSkeem Records and Production is an independent South African media company founded by MVELASE SITHOLE (Mvex Rsa) in 2019. It offers music distribution, filmmaking, voice-over production, photography and PR, with a focus on music videos and recording sessions. Its mission is to support emerging artists and creatives as a one-stop shop for media production.

This page brings together a few favourite Zulu drama movies from Mvex Rsa, a South African creator behind FourSkeem Records and Production.

Isifiso Sami, Mvex's first movie, was released in 2026. A musical group is torn apart by rivalry, betrayal and the cost of chasing fame. Will their relationships survive the cost of success?

Iqiniso

Izinyathelo

Izinyathelo is a powerful Zulu drama about love, family, friendship and difficult choices. The story follows a young woman who constantly disagrees with her younger brother's friendship with an older dance partner.

Uthando

Uthando, a film by Mvex, is the story of a jealous friend who doesn't wish her friend well in her relationship. She's willing to ruin everything because she's jealous that Seven wants her friend, not her, so she's trying by all means to ruin it.
